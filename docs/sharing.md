# Sharing

**Status: stub.** Captured so the thinking isn't lost; needs a session of its own.

Two different jobs that keep getting conflated:

1. **Announcing what just changed** — the migration, the new dedicated site. Small, factual, aimed at people who already follow the work. A changelog entry.
2. **Sharing the work itself** — especially *Without the Hot Air*. Much bigger, aimed at people who have never heard of any of this. A launch.

They want different copy, different venues, and — importantly — **different timing**.

## The prior evidence

The 2021 announcement did well:

| | |
|---|---|
| Post | [Sustainable Energy without the Hot Air (Revised, Community Edition)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=29056343) |
| Date | 2021-10-31 |
| Result | **168 points, 133 comments** |
| Linked to | `climate.lifeitself.us/without-hot-air/` |

For context, the book itself has been posted to HN repeatedly: [71 points in 2009](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=845446), and Chris Goodall's ["keeping it up to date" piece](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=14009057) took **106 points in 2017**. So there is durable, recurring appetite for both the book *and* the idea of updating it — the 2017 thread is the clearest evidence that "someone should refresh these numbers" is itself the story.

That is a strong hand. It is also a hand you can only play well once every few years.

## The strategic point

**Do not spend the launch on "we moved it to a new domain."**

A dedicated site is not, on its own, a story. "We are using AI to bring MacKay's numbers up to date, and here is the first pass" *is* a story — and it lands squarely on the appetite the 2017 thread demonstrated. The strongest version is both together: **new home + a real update in hand**.

Which implies a sequence:

1. Ship the site quietly (**done**)
2. Do the update work — [without-hot-air#1](https://github.com/life-itself/without-hot-air/issues/1)
3. *Then* launch, with something to show

The changelog entry (job 1) can go out now; it costs nothing and burns nothing.

## Open questions

- **Where should the 2021 HN link land?** It points at `climate.lifeitself.us/without-hot-air/`, which still resolves (`.us` → `.org`, 200) but now shows the *project write-up*, not the book. Someone arriving from that thread wants the book. Options: redirect it to withouthotair.org, or leave it as an explainer that links onward. Currently the latter.
- **What venue beyond HN?** The original also went to Reddit. Which subs, and is there a lower-risk place to test the copy first?
- **Does the changelog live on the climate site or the Life Itself blog?** A `/changelog` page here is cheap and version-controlled; the blog has more readers.
- **Who is the audience for the update?** People who loved the book, or people who have never read it? That decides whether the pitch is "refreshed" or "here is the best book on energy, now current."
- **How honest to be about the AI involvement?** HN will ask, and will be unforgiving if it looks like the numbers were generated rather than sourced. Probably an asset if the method and sourcing are shown; a liability if it's a one-line mention. See the trust discussion in [without-hot-air#1](https://github.com/life-itself/without-hot-air/issues/1).
