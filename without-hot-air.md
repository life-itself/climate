title: Sustainable Energy without the Hot Air
---

David MacKay's *Sustainable Energy — Without the Hot Air* — preserved, converted to markdown, and open to revision — now has its own home:

**→ [withouthotair.org](https://withouthotair.org)**

It is a brilliant book and still a go-to reference, despite being published in 2008. MacKay's factual approach — and his "you only understand what you can create for yourself" mentality — shaped this whole climate inquiry.

MacKay died in 2016, so the book will not be revised by him. We converted it to markdown so that it *can* be revised, collaboratively and in the open. The source lives at [github.com/life-itself/without-hot-air](https://github.com/life-itself/without-hot-air).
